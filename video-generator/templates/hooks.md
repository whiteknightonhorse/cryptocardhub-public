# Video Hooks — Opening Lines (0–3 s)

The first 3 seconds decide watch-time. The `write_script` step picks **one hook** per video at random from the matching format's pool.
Always: confident, specific, no filler. No "Hey guys".

---

## Format A — Never Sell Your Crypto

- "What if you could spend your ETH without selling it?"
- "Spending crypto without selling it just got real."
- "Why I stopped selling ETH to pay rent."
- "Touch your ETH. Without selling it."
- "You can spend ETH like cash. Here's how."

## Format B — Replace Your Bank

- "I threw away my bank card 3 months ago."
- "My bank no longer holds my money. This card does."
- "Replace your bank in 2 minutes."
- "Every bank in the world charges you for this. This card pays you."
- "Day 90 without a bank card. Here's what changed."

## Format C — Monthly Cashback

- "$127 in free crypto this month — just from groceries."
- "I get paid in ETH every time I buy coffee."
- "$2,400 in free crypto this year. From normal spending."
- "Your card pays Visa. Mine pays me."
- "I made $89 in crypto last weekend without doing anything."

## Format D — Travel Hack

- "My bank charged me $180 in foreign fees last trip."
- "Tokyo, Lisbon, Bali — same card, no fees."
- "How I saved $400 on one trip with this card."
- "This card has zero foreign-transaction fees. None."
- "I traveled 3 countries last week with one card. Zero fees."

## Format E — Beginner Friendly

- "You don't need to understand DeFi to use this card."
- "If you can use Apple Pay, you can use this."
- "Crypto card. Zero learning curve."
- "Forget DeFi for a second — this is just a card."
- "First crypto product that doesn't require a tutorial."

## Format F — Watch This BEFORE You Get…

- "Watch this BEFORE you get a crypto card."
- "3 mistakes that cost crypto-card users hundreds."
- "Most people set up their crypto card wrong. Here's how to do it right."
- "Don't get a crypto card until you watch this."
- "I made every crypto-card mistake. So you don't have to."

---

## Hook construction rules

1. **Concrete number or specific noun** in the first 7 words.
2. **Curiosity gap** — promise resolution, deliver in beat 2.
3. **No throat-clearing** ("So today I want to talk about…" → cut).
4. **Cut on visual change** — hook ends when the camera/scene changes.
5. **No emoji-style hype** ("🔥 Insane $$$ hack 🔥") — looks AI-generated.

---

## A/B testing

- Generate 2 hooks per video.
- Render twice, post one to YouTube and one to TikTok.
- Track 3-day retention curve, retain the winner pool weekly.
